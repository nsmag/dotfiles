a terminal palette after Claude Monet, Spring Flowers, 1864

Hues are sampled from the painting: geraniums, forget-me-nots, wallflowers, lilac, viburnum and peonies against the umber backdrop. Lightness and chroma are lifted so every accent clears 4.5:1 on the background. Click a swatch to tweak it and the terminals below update live. Hover a swatch to find where it came from.
